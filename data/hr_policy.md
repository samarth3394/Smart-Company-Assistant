# Nexora Inc. — Human Resources Policy Handbook

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 1, 2026

---

## 1. Working Hours and Attendance

All full-time employees are expected to work **40 hours per week**, typically Monday through Friday from **9:00 AM to 5:30 PM** with a 30-minute lunch break. Core collaboration hours are **10:00 AM – 3:00 PM**; outside of these hours, employees may adjust their schedule with their manager's approval.

Employees must clock in using the **Nexora TimePulse app** within 10 minutes of their start time. Three or more unexcused late arrivals within a rolling 30-day period may result in a verbal warning from HR.

## 2. Remote Work Policy

Nexora offers a **hybrid work model**. Employees may work remotely up to **three days per week**, provided their role permits it. Remote work requests must be submitted through the **HR Portal** at least 48 hours in advance. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval and are reviewed quarterly.

All remote employees must maintain a reliable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps download) and be available on Slack and Zoom during core hours. Remote workers are eligible for a one-time **$500 home office stipend** upon completing 90 days of employment.

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue PTO as follows:

- **0–2 years tenure:** 18 days per year
- **3–5 years tenure:** 22 days per year
- **6+ years tenure:** 28 days per year

PTO requests must be submitted at least **5 business days** in advance for planned absences. Unused PTO up to **5 days** may be carried over to the next calendar year; any excess is forfeited on December 31. PTO is paid at the employee's regular daily rate.

## 4. Sick Leave

Employees receive **10 paid sick days** per year, separate from PTO. Sick leave may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for an immediate family member. A doctor's note is required for absences exceeding **3 consecutive days**.

Unused sick days do **not** carry over and are not paid out upon separation from the company.

## 5. Parental Leave

Nexora provides **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave** for the birth or adoption of a child, available to all eligible employees regardless of gender. To qualify, employees must have at least **12 months of continuous service**. An additional **4 weeks of unpaid leave** may be requested with manager approval.

## 6. Performance Reviews

Performance evaluations are conducted **twice a year**: a mid-year check-in in **June** and a comprehensive annual review in **December**. Reviews are based on goal achievement, peer feedback (360-degree review), and alignment with Nexora's core values.

Employees rated "Exceeds Expectations" or above are eligible for an annual bonus of **10–20%** of their base salary. A "Performance Improvement Plan" (PIP) lasting **60 days** is initiated for any employee rated "Below Expectations" in two consecutive review cycles.

## 7. Expense Reimbursement

Business-related expenses are reimbursed within **15 business days** of receipt submission via the **Nexora Expense Hub**. Meals during business travel are capped at **$75/day**. Flights must be booked in economy class unless the flight exceeds 6 hours, in which case premium economy is permitted.

All expenses over **$250** require pre-approval from the employee's direct manager.

## 8. Code of Conduct

Nexora is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive workplace. Discrimination, harassment, and retaliation of any kind are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate termination. All employees must complete **annual ethics and anti-harassment training** by March 31 each year.

Confidential company information must never be shared outside the organization without written authorization from the Legal department.
